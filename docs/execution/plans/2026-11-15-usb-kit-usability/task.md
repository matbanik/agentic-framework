### Evidence-First & Sequencing Rules

- **Evidence-first completion:** a row is `[x]` only when its deliverable exists and the handoff carries its evidence.
- **Red first:** each coder row runs the red command in its Task cell into its `uk2-red-*` receipt before it changes any code, and its validation starts with the `--red` check of that receipt. A listed AC with no failing test in the red receipts is stop condition 5.
- **Human rows:** rows 11a and 12 to 15 are `[B]` human gates until the human asks for them or reports them done. A script that changes the hub or a member runs only when the human asks (stop condition 3). Each row's validation command then decides `[x]`.
- **The review barrier:** the handoff (H1-6) comes before the review, and the H2 artifacts come after `approved`.
- **No self-review:** the implementer dispatches the H1-7 review and never authors its verdict.
- **`[B]` is evidence-gated:** it is allowed only for a reproduced external error, a missing dependency, credential or permission, or a human-decision gate, each with a `### B-<row-id>` block.
- **Valid stops:** the plan's stop conditions, DONE, the review cap, an unavailable reviewer, or a human decision. Compaction continues the pass.
