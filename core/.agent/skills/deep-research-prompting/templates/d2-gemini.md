# Gemini lens — Trends & communities

Output file: `prompt-d2-gemini.md`. Provider: Gemini. Slant: **Trends & communities**.
Attribution tag: `[Gemini]`.

Source of every Portal Setup step: `capabilities/gemini.md` (Model, Portal Affordances,
Constraints). Drop a step when the current snapshot no longer supports it.

## Portal Setup

1. At the bottom of the chat box, open the model picker and choose **Argon** (Gemini 4 Argon;
   AI Pro or Ultra). Without it, choose Pro.
2. Set the thinking level to **High**; the default is Low.
3. Choose **Deep Research**. Keep Google Search as a source; add Gmail or Drive only when the
   topic needs them (Ultra visuals are not available with those sources).
4. Paste the prompt. When the research plan appears, choose Edit plan until it matches
   `## Expected Research Plan`, then start. The run takes about 5–10 minutes; you can leave the
   chat.
5. Export the report to Google Docs and save its text as `output-gemini.md`. Record the model
   shown at the end of "Show thinking steps".

## Objective emphasis

Trends and communities: search-behavior evidence — what people actually query, related-question
clusters, unanswered-question gaps; adoption velocity over time; recency of every signal.

## Deliverable

A Markdown report: a dated trend timeline, a table of the questions people ask with the gap each
reveals, a community map (where the discussion happens and its tone), and the `## Sources`
appendix.

## Expected Research Plan

- Find how search interest and discussion volume changed over the last 24 months.
- Collect the recurring questions and the ones no source answers well.
- Identify the communities (forums, subreddits, Discord, newsletters) and their sentiment.
- Date every signal; prefer the last 6 months.
- Reconcile conflicting signals instead of averaging them.
